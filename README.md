Turn your photos into GTA 6-style cover art — a multi-panel poster maker that runs fully offline in the desktop browser. Load a photo, pick a layout and grade, edit the caption, and export a PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350, or 1920×1080. No uploads, no network, no Rockstar artwork — all art and fonts are original.

## Entry files

`main.ts` copies the static app and screenshots into `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) and writes the make.fun dossier. Screenshots are in `screenshots/`.
